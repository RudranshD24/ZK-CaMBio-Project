| D-007 | 2026-09-30 | Face encoder: InceptionResnetV1 (`facenet-pytorch==2.6.0`, `--no-deps`, pretrained: `vggface2`). Produces 512-d L2-normalized embeddings. Pretrained training data: VGGFace2 (Cao et al., BMVC 2018; 3.31M images across 9,131 identities). Notes: (1) VGGFace2 may have subject overlap with UMDFaces (both web-scraped celebrity sets), which cannot be ruled out and will be disclosed in the paper. Operates natively in PyTorch without degrading dependencies. | ArcFace ONNX via onnxruntime (rejected: external weights hosting dependency), MTCNN re-alignment (rejected: lower separation and redundant on pre-cropped faces) |

| D-009 | 2026-09-30 | Same-DB impostor evaluation protocol for fingerprints & fusion | Cross-DB fingerprint pairs are captured with different sensors (optical, thermal) and trivially separable, creating artificially low impostor scores.

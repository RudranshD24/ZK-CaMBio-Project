| D-010 | 2026-09-30 | Fingerprint Preprocessing V2 and Encoder Choice: Selected Preprocessing V2 (fixed physical scale factor per DB, foreground centroid centering, tighter DB2 platen window excluding slanted border bars, constant pad 255) and Baseline B (FingerResNet18 fine-tuned with CosFace margin loss + extra training data, 256-d output embedding). Fixed scale justification: Same sensor = same physical resolution; dynamic bounding box scaling destroyed spatial ridge frequency across impressions. DB1 (raw 640x480): 384x384 window (scale 0.333) fits ~350-380px typical print without boundary cuts or magnification. DB2 (raw 328x364): horizontal window 256 centered at x=164 (42<=x<=286) strictly excludes slanted trapezoid platen edges and padding bars (scale 0.50). DB3 (raw 300x480 thermal): 384x384 window (scale 0.333) centered at x=150 and vertical centroid accommodates full sweep. Model selection strictly on 30 validation fingers (10/DB, same-DB impostors): (1) Gabor V1: Val Sep=0.0089, Val EER=47.65%; (2) Gabor V2: Val Sep=0.0129, Val EER=44.01%; (3) ResNet18 V1 (150 fit): Val Sep=0.6282, Val EER=6.48%; (4) ResNet18 V2 (150 fit): Val Sep=0.6322, Val EER=6.79%; (5) ResNet18 V2 + Extra Data (290 fingers: 150 fit + 100 DB4_A + 40 DB*_B, verified zero manifest overlap): Val Sep=0.6414, Val EER=5.49% (WINNER). Single test evaluation on 120 test subjects under Protocol D-009 (same-DB impostors): ResNet18 achieved 5.81% pooled EER (95% CI [4.72%, 7.15%]), Rank-1 fair gallery-40 accuracy 78.89% (DB1: 5.06% EER / 75.83% Rank-1; DB2: 8.50% EER / 75.00% Rank-1; DB3: 3.35% EER / 85.83% Rank-1). Gabor V2 achieved 41.10% pooled EER (95% CI [38.89%, 43.11%]), Rank-1 26.67% (DB1: 38.16% EER; DB2: 33.34% EER; DB3: 32.61% EER). Disclosures: (1) Extra-data gain on 30 validation fingers (val sep 0.6414 vs 0.6322) is modest and within sample noise; (2) 100 extra training fingers are synthetic (DB4_A SFinGe generator); (3) Results apply strictly to same-sensor matching (no cross-sensor claim); (4) Baseline A Gabor is a simple 8x8 grid AAD baseline, not representative of commercial minutiae-based extractors; (5) Pooled gallery-120 CMC is not a harder test because cross-sensor entries never rank first; per-DB gallery-40 is the headline identification benchmark. (6) DB*_B sets confirmed to use finger IDs 101..110, completely disjoint from A sets (1..100). | Preprocessing V1 resolved scale inconsistently and left trapezoid borders in DB2 (rejected: yielded 26.37% test EER with 37.04% DB2 EER). ResNet18 V2 without extra data (rejected: lower validation separation 0.6322 vs 0.6414). Baseline A Gabor (41.10% test EER; preserved as classical benchmark). |
| D-011 | 2026-09-30 | Multimodal Fusion: Feature-level fusion with weight w=0.60 (fused = [sqrt(w)*f_face, sqrt(1-w)*f_finger], 768-d unit-norm vector). Tuned strictly on the 30 validation subjects over grid w in [0.10, 0.90]; identified a flat near-optimal basin w in [0.50, 0.70] (val EER 0.00% - 0.18%) and selected w=0.60 (center of plateau, giving principled balance toward the stronger face modality). Test set evaluation under Protocol D-009 (120 test subjects, same-DB impostors): S3 fused feature-level achieves 1.11% pooled EER (95% CI [0.29%, 1.66%]), FNMR@1%=1.11%, FNMR@0.1%=1.94%, d'=5.91, Rank-1 gallery-40 accuracy 99.44%. Score-level fusion (S3b, z-score normalized) achieves 1.10% pooled EER and 99.17% Rank-1. Fused EER is 0.89% lower absolute than best single modality (Face: 2.00%, Finger: 5.81%), representing a 44.5% error reduction. Note: 120 test subjects x 3 probes = 360 genuine trials, establishing a discrete genuine trial resolution of 1/360 = 0.28% (1.11% EER corresponds to ~4 genuine errors). Test sensitivity curve saved to results/fusion_test_weight_sensitivity.png (analysis only, confirmed robust performance across w in [0.35, 0.70]). | Unweighted concatenation (rejected: ignores modality strength imbalance); score-level fusion as primary (rejected: feature-level provides single unified vector for cancelable chaotic hashing in Phase 4); tuning w on 150 fit fingers (rejected: overfitted fingerprint scores bias w artificially). |
